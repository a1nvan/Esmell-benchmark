# Raw measurement archives

Download the raw archives from the companion dataset linked in the paper. Place the following files in `data/raw/` or point `ESMELL_DATA_DIR` to their directory, preserving the basenames:

```text
curiosity-1v4.129.0.tar.gz
curiosity-2v15.130.0.tar.gz
curiosity-2v15.146.0.tar.gz
curiosity-2v15.157.0.tar.gz
curiosity-2v15.166.0.tar.gz
curiosity-2v15.169.0.tar.gz
curiosity-5v16.171.0.tar.gz
curiosity-1v17.172.0.tar.gz
```

Check the archive hashes against `archive_checksums.sha256`. Campaign 157 is included in the dataset but is not required by the E1–E7 notebooks. Campaigns 120 and 122 are excluded because of confirmed gas-delivery faults.
